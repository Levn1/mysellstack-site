Questions

Frequently asked

Is the demo on this page the real widget?

Yes. It is the exact JavaScript bundle our theme extension ships to storefronts, running against sample data instead of a live shop. What you see is what your customers get.

What does it cost?

Free covers one service with unlimited bookings and 30 days of history. Standard ($12/month) raises that to 20 services with reminders and branded emails; Pro ($24/month) to 40 services with Google Calendar sync, Meet links, group events, and upsells; Ultra ($36/month) to 60 services with Outlook and Teams, your own sending domain, and CSV exports. Ultimate ($88/month) removes the limits: unlimited services and unlimited history. Paid plans carry a 7-day free trial, charges land on your regular Shopify invoice, and no plan caps your bookings or your revenue. During early access everything is free.

Do paid bookings really go through Shopify checkout?

Yes. A paid service books through your store’s own checkout, so taxes, payment methods, discounts, and order history work the way they already do. Free and pay-on-arrival services book instantly without payment.

Will it slow my storefront down?

No. The widget ships in a Shopify theme app extension served from Shopify’s CDN, and its script loads deferred so it never blocks your page from rendering. Booking UI appears only on booking pages.

How do group classes fill up?

You set the capacity per class. Slots show how many places are left, stop selling when full, and can run at fixed times with one or several teachers.

Can customers cancel or reschedule on their own?

On the Pro, Ultra, and Ultimate plans, every booking email links to a secure page where the customer can reschedule or cancel within the notice windows you set. On any plan, you can reschedule or cancel any booking from the admin yourself.

What does the app do with a connected Google Calendar?

Two things. It creates and updates calendar events for bookings, including Google Meet links on virtual services, and it reads events on the calendars each staff member picks so busy times can’t be double-booked. Calendar data is used for nothing else: no ads, no selling, no unrelated processing. Access ends the moment you disconnect the calendar in the app or revoke it in your Google account. MySellStack Appointments’ use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What about my customers’ data?

Booking data is processed only to run your bookings. It is never sold and never used for advertising. Erasure requests, retention limits, and encrypted backups are built in. Read the privacy policy.

About the app

What MySellStack Appointments does

MySellStack Appointments is a Shopify app that turns products in your store into bookable services. Customers pick a time on your storefront and pay in your own checkout, while the app runs the calendar behind it: staff schedules, group classes, confirmations, reminders, and self-serve rescheduling.

If you connect a Google account, the app uses your Google Calendar data for one purpose only: it creates and updates calendar events for your bookings and reads busy times on the calendars you choose, so booked slots never collide with your existing commitments. Calendar data is never sold and never used for advertising, and disconnecting the calendar ends the app’s access. How all data is handled is described in the privacy policy.
