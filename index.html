Questions

Frequently asked

Do paid bookings really go through Shopify checkout?

Yes. A paid service books through your store’s own checkout, so tax settings, payment methods, discounts, and order history all work the way they already do. Free and pay-on-arrival services skip payment and book instantly.

Will it slow my storefront down?

The widget ships inside a Shopify theme app extension, served from Shopify’s CDN, and its script loads deferred so it never blocks your page from rendering. Booking UI appears only on booking pages.

Can customers cancel or reschedule on their own?

Yes — every booking email links to a secure page where the customer can reschedule or cancel within the notice windows you configure. You can also do either from the admin at any time.

What about my customers’ data?

Booking data is processed only to run your bookings — never sold, never used for advertising. Erasure requests, retention limits, and encrypted backups are built in. Read the privacy policy.
