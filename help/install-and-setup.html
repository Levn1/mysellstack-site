Install & first-time setup

Installing takes one click from the Shopify App Store, and the app opens right inside your Shopify admin. Here’s what you’re looking at, and the three steps between you and your first booking.

Your dashboard

The app opens on the Calendar page, your home base. From top to bottom:

  • The status card: shows whether your booking widget is live on the storefront, and holds the Manual booking button for phone and walk-in customers.
  • Analytics: revenue, bookings, views, and conversion, with selectable time ranges and CSV export.
  • The calendar: every booking across services and staff, in month, week, or day view.

Three steps to your first booking

  1. Create a service: what customers book: price, duration, staff, and schedule. Saving it automatically creates a matching product in your store.
  2. Turn the widget on in your theme: one app-embed toggle in the theme editor. Your service’s product page swaps “Add to cart” for “Book now”.
  3. Make a test booking: open the service’s product page on your storefront, pick a slot, and book it. It appears on your dashboard calendar and sends a confirmation email.
Testing payments? On a development store, use Shopify’s Bogus Gateway to pay for a test booking with the card number 1. On a live store, you can create a free service to test without money moving.

Worth doing early

  • Connect your calendar so bookings land where you already look, and busy time blocks your slots.
  • Set your hours, breaks, and closed dates so only real availability is offered.
  • Skim the email templates. They work out of the box, but your logo and wording make them yours.
